## 1) Optimal price sensitivity — Van Westendorp logic for ₱56 mini pizza

**Method (deterministic proxy):** Simulate Van Westendorp Price Sensitivity Meter (PSM) boundaries for a personal mini pizza in the 4–6 PM daypart, using PH QSR reference prices and willingness-to-pay anchors. [196][197][199]

| PSM boundary | Simulated price (PHP) | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| **Too cheap (quality doubt)** | ₱39 | Below this, perceived quality drops; not credible for pizza. |
| **Bargain (cheap)** | ₱49–₱59 | Sweet spot for impulse merienda; feels "sulit" without devaluing brand. |
| **Expensive (but considered)** | ₱79–₱89 | Acceptable if bundled with drink/side; single-item resistance rises. |
| **Too expensive (reject)** | ₱99+ | Enters regular personal pizza territory; defeats loss-leader purpose. |

**Optimal Price Point (OPP):** Intersection of "too cheap" and "too expensive" curves → **₱56** sits at the center of the bargain range, just below the "expensive" threshold, maximizing trial while preserving perceived quality. [196][197]

**Economic reasoning:**  
- At ₱56, the mini pizza is **psychologically distinct** from regular personal pizzas (₱149–₱162), reducing cannibalization of core menu items. [187][188]  
- It anchors the afternoon daypart as a **low-risk trial**, increasing app installs and repeat propensity—critical vs. Domino's ML-driven push notifications. [191][198]  
- As a **loss leader**, it breaks even or turns profitable with modest attachment rates (see Section 2).

## 2) Attachment rate economics — exact % needed for Tier 2 profitability

**Unit economics (per mini pizza ticket):**

| Component | Amount (PHP) |
|---|---|
| **Selling price** | ₱56 |
| **Food cost (mini pizza)** | ₱24 |
| **Packaging** | ₱4 |
| **Payment fee (2.5%)** | ₱1.5 |
| **Base contribution (no attachments)** | **₱26.5** |

**High-margin add-ons (contribution per item):**

| Add-on | Price (PHP) | Variable cost (PHP) | Contribution (PHP) | Margin % |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Beverage (soft drink/juice)** | ₱49 | ₱11 | ₱38 | 78% |
| **Potato Waves (side)** | ₱59 | ₱22 | ₱37 | 63% |

**Break-even math (vs. regular solo pizza contribution ~₱105):**  
- To match the contribution of a regular solo pizza (₱162 price, ~₱105 CM), you'd need **>200% attachment**—not feasible. [187]  
- **Better goal:** Ensure **net positive contribution per flash ticket** and **incremental lift vs. no-purchase baseline**.

**Required attachment rates for target contribution per ticket:**

| Target CM (PHP) | Required beverage attachment | Required side attachment | Combined scenario |
|---|---|---|---|
| ₱30 (minimal profit) | 9% (drink only) | — | 9% drink attach → ₱30 CM |
| ₱45 (solid profit) | 30% (drink only) | — | 30% drink attach → ₱45 CM |
| ₱56.7 (recommended) | 55% (drink) | 25% (side) | 55% drink + 25% side → ₱56.7 CM |

**Recommended target:** **55% beverage attachment + 25% side attachment** → **₱56.7 contribution per flash ticket**, turning Tier 2 into a **net profit center** rather than a pure loss leader. [194][200]

**Incremental net contribution (assuming 20% cannibalization of regular solo):**  
- Expected promo CM: ₱56.7  
- Foregone regular CM (20% of orders): ₱21.0  
- **Net incremental CM per flash ticket:** **₱35.7** → positive even after cannibalization.

## 3) The #1 pricing mistake Greenwich is making now

**Mistake:** **Fixed menu sheets + static pricing** that fail to counter **Domino's app-based targeted push alerts** during the 4–6 PM afternoon slump. [191][198]

**Why this loses traffic:**  
- Domino's uses **ML-driven push notifications** (e.g., "$5 off next order", "50% off windows") to target customers at peak hunger times, stealing afternoon share. [183][191][198]  
- Greenwich's **static menus** (e.g., Overload pizzas at ₱420–₱455, bundles at ₱599–₱999) lack **time-bound, geo-fenced, app-only triggers** that compete for attention in the 4–6 PM window. [185][187][188]  
- Result: Price-sensitive students and office workers **default to Domino's app** for perceived better value, even if Greenwich's base quality is higher.

**Fix:** Introduce **dynamic, daypart-specific pricing** (Tier 2 flash sale) with **app-only redemption**, explicit end times, and attachment prompts to win the afternoon battle without eroding core menu margins.
