# Customer Personas — Greenwich Pizza Initiatives, Philippines 2026

## Overview
Three data-backed consumer personas for Greenwich's growth initiatives:
1) **Student Combo (₱149)** — targets "Budget Maximizer" students  
2) **Flash Sale (4–6 PM, ₱129)** — targets "Meryenda Hustler" office workers  
3) **Kids + Family Bundle (₱179–₱199)** — targets "Family Occasion Planner" parents

Each persona is built from behavioral patterns in PH QSR, delivery app usage, snacking culture, and Gen Z trust dynamics—not generic demographics. Each points to a **different primary acquisition channel**.

---

## Persona 1 — "Budget Maximizer" (Student Combo)

### Behavioral signature
- Spends ~40% of allowance on food & drinks; daily baon ₱100–₱150 for lunch + merienda. [171][172]  
- Eats fast food 3–5x/week; heavily uses GrabFood/Foodpanda for convenience and promos. [149][151]  
- Discovers food via TikTok/Instagram Reels; trusts nano/mid-tier creators and peer reviews over celebrities. [158][160][166]

### Psychographics
- Value-conscious but socially driven—wants "sulit" (worth it) meals that also look good on Stories.  
- Treats merienda as a social ritual with classmates; price sensitivity high but willing to stretch budget for perceived value. [175][177]

### What they read/follow/trust
- TikTok food creators (local, authentic reviews), campus org pages, peer group chats (Messenger/Viber). [159][161]  
- App push notifications from GrabFood/Foodpanda with "student" tags or campus geo-fencing. [149][165]

### Buying trigger
- **Event:** "Allowance day" or post-exam treat; sees a student combo promo on TikTok or app banner.  
- **Decision process:** Checks app price vs. canteen; asks barkada (friend group) in chat if it's worth it; orders if combo < ₱150 with drink. [171][174]  
- **Influencers:** Nano campus foodies (0–10k followers) with high relatability; barkada consensus. [158][166]

### Objection (top reason to say no)
- "Baka mahal 'yun kahit student discount—may hidden fees sa delivery." (Might be expensive even with student discount—hidden delivery fees.) [167]

### Exact phrases for ad copy
- "Student meal na ₱149 na may drink—sulit, 'di ba?"  
- "Baon-friendly pizza combo, perfect sa merienda with barkada."  
- "No hidden fees, student price lang—order na sa app!"

### Primary acquisition channel
**TikTok + App Push (campus geo-fenced)** — nano-influencer reviews + time-bound student combo banners in GrabFood/Foodpanda during 2–5 PM. [149][158][165]

## Persona 2 — "Meryenda Hustler" (Flash Sale 4–6 PM)

### Behavioral signature
- Office worker or BPO; 30% seek energy-boosting afternoon snacks; 95% don't skip afternoon merienda. [147][148]  
- Orders food 2–4x/week between 3–6 PM; uses GrabFood for speed, Foodpanda for promos. [151][156]  
- Late-night eating common (7 in 10 Filipinos); price-sensitive, spends ₱200–₱400 per order. [149]

### Psychographics
- Functional snacker—needs quick energy to power through late shift or finish tasks.  
- Impulse-driven but promo-sensitive; values speed and convenience over brand loyalty.

### What they read/follow/trust
- GrabFood/Foodpanda app banners ("Flash Sale 4–6 PM"), SMS promos from QSRs, workplace group chats. [149][165]  
- Google Maps reviews for "fast delivery" and "hot pizza" during afternoon slump.

### Buying trigger
- **Event:** 3:30 PM energy dip; sees SMS or app push: "₱129 personal pizza + 1 topping, ends 6 PM."  
- **Decision process:** Compares delivery time (35–42 min avg) vs. walking to food court; orders if ETA < 40 min and total < ₱200. [156][167]  
- **Influencers:** Peer recommendations in workplace chat; app ratings (4.5+ stars).

### Objection (top reason to say no)
- "Baka cold na pagdating, or tagal ng delivery—mas worth it kung fresh at mabilis." (Might arrive cold, or delivery too slow—only worth it if fresh and fast.)

### Exact phrases for ad copy
- "4–6 PM flash sale: ₱129 personal pizza + 1 topping, delivered hot in 40 mins."  
- "Meryenda na 'di nagpa-pause sa work—order na, bago maubusan!"  
- "Ends 6 PM lang—sulit merienda for your shift."

### Primary acquisition channel
**SMS + App Push (time-bound)** — 3:15 PM and 5:00 PM blasts with explicit end time; GrabFood/Foodpanda banner during 4–6 PM window. [149][165][167]

## Persona 3 — "Family Occasion Planner" (Kids + Family Bundle)

### Behavioral signature
- Parent (mom or dad) planning weekend family meals; checks restaurant hygiene, halal claims, and sharing sizes before ordering. [154]  
- Orders family bundles 1–2x/month; spends ₱400–₱800 per order; prefers cash on delivery (61% of Filipinos). [149]  
- Uses Facebook for restaurant research; trusts parent group recommendations and local food bloggers.

### Psychographics
- Risk-averse—prioritizes kids' safety (allergies, hygiene) and value for money.  
- Emotional driver: "Quality time with family" on weekends; wants meals that feel special but affordable.

### What they read/follow/trust
- Facebook parent groups (barangay-level), local food bloggers with family content, restaurant permits/hygiene posts. [154][160]  
- Google Maps reviews with photos of actual portions and kids' reactions.

### Buying trigger
- **Event:** Weekend family dinner planning (Friday night or Saturday morning); sees Facebook post: "Kids ear-pizza bundle ₱179 with side + drink + dessert."  
- **Decision process:** Checks menu for sharing sizes, allergen info, and delivery branch proximity; asks spouse or parent group for recs. [154]  
- **Influencers:** Micro parent influencers (5–50k followers) with authentic family content; barangay group admins. [161][166]

### Objection (top reason to say no)
- "Baka maliit yung portion, or may hidden charges—mas okay kung clear ang lahat bago order." (Portion might be too small, or hidden charges—better if everything's clear before ordering.)

### Exact phrases for ad copy
- "Kids ear-pizza bundle: ₱179 na may side, drink, at dessert—perfect for family weekend!"  
- "Clear portions, no hidden fees—family meal na sulit at safe for kids."  
- "Order sa branch na malapit sa inyo—fresh at mabilis delivery."

### Primary acquisition channel
**Facebook + Parent Groups** — targeted posts in barangay parent groups, micro parent influencer reviews, Google Maps local pack ads. [154][160][161]

## How to use these personas

1) **Ad copy:** Use exact phrases in TikTok scripts, SMS blasts, and Facebook posts.  
2) **Channel mix:** Allocate budget per persona's primary channel (TikTok, SMS, Facebook).  
3) **Landing pages:** Tailor student combo page for mobile-first TikTok traffic; flash sale page for app deep-links; family bundle page for Facebook/Google Maps.  
4) **A/B tests:** Test objection-handling variants (e.g., "No hidden fees" vs. "Free delivery" for students; "Hot in 40 mins" vs. "Fast delivery" for office workers).  
5) **KPIs:** Track CTR, conversion rate, and repeat rate by persona/channel; optimize based on behavioral response, not just demographics.

These personas are grounded in PH-specific behavioral data: baon budgets, merienda culture, delivery app dominance, Gen Z trust in nano-influencers, and family dining risk aversion. Use them to tailor ad copy, channel mix, and promo mechanics for each initiative.
