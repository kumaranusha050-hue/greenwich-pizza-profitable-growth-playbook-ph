# Market Sizing (TAM / SAM / SOM) — Greenwich Pizza Initiatives, Philippines 2026

## Objective
Size the addressable market for Greenwich's three growth initiatives:
1) Student Combo (₱149 personal meal, 2–5 PM weekdays)  
2) Flash Sale (4–6 PM, Wed & Fri, app-only personal + 1 topping, ₱99–₱149)

We present **top-down** (category-led) and **bottom-up** (segment-led) views, flag weak assumptions, and compare to known market reports. All figures are annual, 2026 PHP and USD (₱56 = $1 approx).

- **TAM (pizza+pasta QSR):** $12.20B × 12% = $1.46B → ₱82.0B  
- **SAM (targetable metro Luzon/Visayas):** TAM × urban (47%) × metro (45%) = ₱17.3B  
- **SOM (Greenwich obtainable):** SAM × local pizza leader share (65%) × capture (50%) = ₱5.6B

### Numbers (PHP, annual)
- **TAM:** ₱82.0B  
- **SAM:** ₱17.3B

### Assumptions & sources
- Foodservice 2026: $21.04B; QSR 58% → $12.20B [86][90][92]

## Bottom-up market sizing (segment-led)

### Target segments & mechanics
1) **Students (15–24)** — after-class meals, 2–5 PM weekdays  
2) **Office workers (4–6 PM)** — snack/early dinner, Wed & Fri focus  
3) **Families with kids** — weekend family occasions, bundle-driven

### Formulas
- **Population 2026:** 117.7M [120]  
- **Students 15–24:** 19% of pop → 22.36M; targetable (urban 47% × metro 45%) → 4.71M  
- **Office workers:** 67.1% working-age × 60% formal/office → 47.5M; targetable → 10.0M  
- **Families:** Households = pop / 4.5 → 26.16M; 55% with kids → 14.39M; targetable → 3.03M

- **Average tickets:** Student ₱149; Flash ₱129; Family bundle ₱399 [29][53][77]  
- **Frequency (annual):** Students 24x; Office 20x; Families 18x

- **TAM (segment):** targetable population × frequency × ticket  
- **SAM:** sum of three segment TAMs  
- **SOM (Greenwich):** SAM × local pizza leader share (65%) × capture (60%) = ₱25.2B

### Numbers (PHP, annual)
- **TAM Students:** ₱16.9B  
- **TAM Office 4–6 PM:** ₱25.9B  
- **TAM Families:** ₱21.9B  
- **SAM (sum):** ₱64.6B  
- **SOM (Greenwich):** ₱25.2B

### Assumptions & sources
- Pop 117.7M; age splits from PH demographics [120][139]  
- Tickets anchored to Greenwich student combo (₱149) and typical family pizza deals (₱399) [29][53][77]  
- Frequency conservative vs. QSR norms (1.5–2x/month)  
- Greenwich capture 60% of SAM (strong local brand, store density) [137][138]

### Weaknesses (bottom-up)
- **Office 4–6 PM demand:** Formal/office share (60%) is a proxy; actual pizza daypart demand may be lower without delivery density.  
- **Family ticket (₱399):** Anchor based on family pizza deals; actual AOV varies by cluster. [77][80]  
- **Greenwich capture (60%):** Assumes limited competitive response; Domino's heavy discounting could pressure share. [81][84][85]

## Reality check: is the market smaller than it looks?

Yes—if you define TAM as "all pizza spend in PH," you'll overstate. The **actionable TAM** for Greenwich's initiatives is the **bottom-up behavioral TAM (₱64.6B)**, not the full pizza+pasta category (₱82B). More importantly, the **SOM (₱5.6–25.2B)** is the realistic 12–18 month ceiling for Greenwich, and the lower end (₱5.6B) is more comparable to conservative QSR benchmarks. [89][94][98]

**Recommendation:** Use **₱25.2B (bottom-up SOM)** as the base-case planning number for your three initiatives. Stress-test with **₱5.6B (top-down SOM)** as a downside scenario to show investors you understand execution constraints.

## How to use these numbers in your initiative model

- **Revenue potential per initiative:** Allocate SOM (₱25.2B) across students, office, families based on pilot results.  
- **Contribution margin:** Apply food cost % (28–32% pizza), packaging (₱10–₱20), payment fees (1.5–2.5%), delivery subsidy (₱20–₱40) to compute contribution/order. [67][68][76]  
- **Cannibalization:** Adjust net contribution by estimated cannibalization rates (flash sale 10–25%; student 5–15%; family 5–10%). [68][77]  
- **KPIs:** Track contribution/order, new app sign-ups, 30-day repeat, family ticket lift, kids' bundle attachment. [74][77][78]

## Appendix: Calculation details (for transparency)

### Top-down
- Foodservice 2026: $21.04B → QSR 58% = $12.20B → PHP ₱683.2B  
- Pizza+pasta 12% of QSR → ₱82.0B (TAM)  
- SAM = TAM × 0.47 (urban) × 0.45 (metro) = ₱17.3B  
- SOM = SAM × 0.65 (local leader) × 0.50 (capture) = ₱5.6B

### Bottom-up
- Students: 117.7M × 0.19 × 0.47 × 0.45 = 4.71M × 24 × ₱149 = ₱16.9B  
- Office: 117.7M × 0.671 × 0.60 × 0.47 × 0.45 = 10.0M × 20 × ₱129 = ₱25.9B  
- Families: (117.7M / 4.5) × 0.55 × 0.47 × 0.45 = 3.03M × 18 × ₱399 = ₱21.9B  
- SAM = ₱16.9B + ₱25.9B + ₱21.9B = ₱64.6B  
- SOM = ₱64.6B × 0.65 × 0.60 = ₱25.2B
