# Consumer Research Survey — Greenwich Pizza 2026 Playbook Validation (Philippines)

## Survey purpose
Validate three core hypotheses against actual behaviors of pizza consumers in the Philippines:
1. **Students** will shift weekday afternoon group dining to Greenwich with a targeted 5–6-person Barkada combo discount.  
2. **Corporate professionals** face delivery app fee fatigue and will walk in-store for a ₱56 midweek merienda mini pizza flash sale.  
3. **Parents** will choose an experiential, game-led in-store kids' menu over delivery options with plastic toys.

## Operational parameters
- **12 questions total** (including 2 screening questions).  
- **Strictly behavioral questions** for Q3–Q11 (what respondents **actually did/spent** in the last 14 days).  
- **1 choice-based conjoint analysis question** (Q10) to test willingness-to-pay (WTP) trade-offs between Greenwich dining deals and Domino's delivery app convenience.  
- **Under 4 minutes** completion time for local consumers.  
- **95% confidence level, 5% margin of error** for urban Philippines QSR pizza market.

---

## Sample size calculation

**Formula:**  
\[
n = \frac{Z^2 \cdot p \cdot (1-p)}{E^2}
\]

Where:  
- \( n \) = required sample size  
- \( Z \) = Z-score for 95% confidence level (1.96)  
- \( p \) = estimated population proportion (0.5 for maximum variability)  
- \( E \) = margin of error (0.05)

**Calculation:**  
\[
n = \frac{1.96^2 \cdot 0.5 \cdot (1-0.5)}{0.05^2} = \frac{3.8416 \cdot 0.25}{0.0025} = \frac{0.9604}{0.0025} = 384.16
\]

**Required sample size:** **385 respondents** (rounded up from 384.16).

**Population context:**  
- Urban Philippines QSR pizza consumers (Metro Manila, Cebu, Davao, Clark).  
- Estimated population: ~10M urban pizza consumers (conservative).  
- Finite population correction not needed (population >> sample size).

## Survey questions

### Q1 (Screening — pizza consumption)
**Question text:**  
"In the last 14 days, how many times did you order or eat pizza (from any restaurant, including delivery, dine-in, or pickup)?"  

**Answer options:**  
- 0 times → **Terminate survey**  
- 1–2 times  
- 3–5 times  
- 6 or more times  

**What this gives me:**  
Screens for **actual pizza consumers** (not hypothetical interest); ensures respondents have **recent behavioral experience** with pizza ordering/eating.  

**Potential bias & mitigation:**  
- **Recall bias**: Respondents may under/over-report. Mitigation: Use "last 14 days" (short recall window) and provide concrete examples (delivery, dine-in, pickup).  
- **Social desirability bias**: Respondents may over-report to appear engaged. Mitigation: Anonymous survey, neutral wording ("how many times" not "how often do you enjoy").

---

### Q2 (Screening — brand exposure)
**Question text:**  
"In the last 14 days, which pizza brands did you order from or eat at? (Select all that apply)"  

**Answer options:**  
- Greenwich  
- Domino's  
- Pizza Hut  
- Yellow Cab  
- Other (please specify)  
- None of these → **Terminate survey**  

**What this gives me:**  
Confirms **brand exposure** to Greenwich and/or Domino's; enables **segmentation** (Greenwich users, Domino's users, both).  

**Potential bias & mitigation:**  
- **Brand recall bias**: Respondents may forget brands. Mitigation: Provide common brand list + "Other" option.  
- **Termination bias**: Excluding "None" respondents ensures **relevant sample** but may miss potential switchers. Mitigation: Accept "Other" brands for broader insights.

---

### Q3 (Behavioral — student group dining)
**Question text:**  
"In the last 14 days, on a weekday afternoon (2–5 PM), how many times did you eat pizza with a group of 3 or more friends/classmates (Barkada)?"  

**Answer options:**  
- 0 times  
- 1 time  
- 2–3 times  
- 4 or more times  

**What this gives me:**  
Baseline **frequency of student group dining** in target daypart; identifies **high-frequency segment** for Tier 1 combo.  

**Potential bias & mitigation:**  
- **Recall bias**: Mitigation: "Last 14 days" + specific daypart (2–5 PM).  
- **Definition ambiguity**: "Barkada" may vary in size. Mitigation: Specify "3 or more friends/classmates."

---

### Q4 (Behavioral — student channel preference)
**Question text:**  
"Think about the last time you ate pizza with your Barkada on a weekday afternoon (2–5 PM). Where did you eat it?"  

**Answer options:**  
- At a restaurant (dine-in)  
- In a university hallway/classroom/dorm (delivery/pickup)  
- At home (delivery/pickup)  
- Other (please specify)  

**What this gives me:**  
Actual **channel preference** (dine-in vs. delivery/pickup) for student groups; validates **assumption #1** (preference for physical seating).  

**Potential bias & mitigation:**  
- **Recall bias**: Mitigation: "Last time" (single event, easier to recall).  
- **Social desirability bias**: Respondents may over-report dine-in. Mitigation: Anonymous survey, neutral options.

---

### Q6 (Behavioral — delivery fee fatigue)
**Question text:**  
"In the last 14 days, how many times did you decide NOT to order food delivery for merienda/snack because of the delivery fee or minimum order requirement?"  

**Answer options:**  
- 0 times  
- 1 time  
- 2–3 times  
- 4 or more times  

**What this gives me:**  
Quantifies **delivery fee fatigue**; validates **assumption #2** (professionals avoid delivery due to fees).  

**Potential bias & mitigation:**  
- **Recall bias**: Mitigation: "Last 14 days" + specific reason (delivery fee/minimum).  
- **Attribution bias**: Respondents may blame fees for other reasons (e.g., time). Mitigation: Neutral wording ("because of the delivery fee or minimum order").

### Q9 (Behavioral — kids' meal choice driver)

**Answer options:**

- Other (please specify)  

**What this gives me:**  
Actual **decision driver** for parents; validates **assumption #3** (experience > toys).

- **Recall bias**: Mitigation: "Last time" (single event).  
- **Social desirability bias**: Parents may over-report "food quality" vs. "toys." Mitigation: Anonymous survey, neutral options.

---

### Q10 (Conjoint analysis — WTP trade-offs)
**Question text:**  
"Imagine you want to order pizza for yourself and 2–3 friends on a weekday afternoon (2–5 PM). Which option would you choose?"  

**Answer options (choice-based conjoint):**  
- **Option A:** Greenwich dine-in: 2 large pizzas + 4 drinks for ₱599 (saves ₱150 vs. regular price), eat at restaurant with group seating.  
- **Option B:** Domino's delivery: 2 large pizzas + 4 drinks for ₱649 (includes delivery), delivered to your location in 35–40 mins.  
- **Option C:** Greenwich pickup: 2 large pizzas + 4 drinks for ₱549 (saves ₱200 vs. regular price), pick up at restaurant in 15 mins.  
- **Option D:** I would not order pizza in this situation.  

**What this gives me:**  
Explicit **WTP trade-offs** between **Greenwich dining deals** (dine-in/pickup) and **Domino's delivery convenience**; quantifies **price sensitivity** and **channel preference**.  

**Potential bias & mitigation:**  
- **Hypothetical bias**: Conjoint is inherently hypothetical. Mitigation: Use realistic prices based on actual menus; frame as "imagine" not "would you."  
- **Option complexity**: Too many attributes may confuse. Mitigation: Limit to 3 attributes (price, channel, time); pre-test with 5–10 respondents.

- **Social desirability bias**: Respondents may over-report loyalty. Mitigation: Anonymous survey, neutral wording.

### Q12 (Behavioral — spend amount)
**Question text:**  
"In the last 14 days, approximately how much money (in Philippine pesos) did you spend on pizza (including delivery fees, drinks, sides)?"  

**Answer options:**  
- Less than ₱500  
- ₱500–₱999  
- ₱1,000–₱1,999  
- ₱2,000 or more  
- Prefer not to say  

**What this gives me:**  
Actual **spend level** for pizza; enables **revenue modeling** and **segmentation by spend tier**.  

**Potential bias & mitigation:**  
- **Recall bias**: Mitigation: "Last 14 days" (short window).  
- **Privacy concern**: Respondents may not want to disclose spend. Mitigation: "Prefer not to say" option; anonymous survey.
